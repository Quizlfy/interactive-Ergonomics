## 2024-05-15 - Hide decorative emojis and avatar initials from screen readers
**Learning:** When using emojis or generated avatar initials purely for visual decoration (like icons on cards, or an avatar circle right next to the written-out name), screen readers will announce them, adding noise and potentially confusing users who expect meaningful content.
**Action:** Apply `aria-hidden="true"` to purely decorative visual elements to ensure a cleaner, more focused screen reader experience.
