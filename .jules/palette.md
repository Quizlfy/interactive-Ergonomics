## 2024-05-17 - Reduce Screen Reader Noise with aria-hidden
**Learning:** Purely decorative visual elements like generated avatar initials or decorative emojis create redundant or noisy output for screen readers. In the case of avatars, the initials are read out immediately before the person's full name, which is repetitive and confusing. Emojis used for purely decorative reasons inside cards also add unnecessary noise.
**Action:** Always apply `aria-hidden="true"` to purely decorative visual elements (like `.avatar` initials and decorative `.icon` emojis) when the surrounding context already conveys the necessary information.
